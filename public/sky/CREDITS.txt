Adaptations: resized, brightness reduced, remapped to a celestial sphere in galactic coordinates. Photographic survey background; not a live feed, weather prediction, or simulation of naked-eye brightness.
Galactic coordinate transformation: Astronomy Engine Rotation_GAL_EQJ.
Pleiades center (J2000): RA 03h47m, Dec +24d07m; https://messier.lamost.org/seds/seds.org/messier/en/m/m045.html
Wide mode: virtual 90-degree sky explorer, independent of the 1500-mm telescope and Barlow optics.
Photo export: local rendering of the current simulated eyepiece view, with observation time and direction.

Wide horizon: gambar 1.PNG (Winds Space foreground) and gambar 2.PNG (mountain background), supplied by the Winds Space owner. The original transparent images are stored as winds-horizon-foreground.png and winds-horizon-mountains.png. Both strips wrap 360 degrees, with separate ground baselines and seam blending. The orientation and virtual layer distances are illustrative, not georeferenced. Gentle perspective parallax uses a periodic observer offset around the telescope mount; the mountain layer is ten times farther than the foreground layer. Reduced-motion preferences disable this movement.
Atmosphere: simplified clear-sky colour and visibility model based on current solar elevation at Pangkep. No live weather or physical scattering/refraction simulation. Twilight reference: https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/RST_defs

Horizon contrast: a uniform altitude-only mist gradient; no localized halo or height-dependent fog texture.
